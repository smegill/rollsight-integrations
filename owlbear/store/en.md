# RollSight

Request physical dice and share public RollSight cards through a native Owlbear extension.

See public results in shared Owlbear popups, open collapsed replays in RollSight, and share session RollStats with an eligible plan.

Use supported Forge sheet actions with physical dice and optionally share result summaries in Rumble chat.

A Windows or Mac computer, a compatible camera, and a rolling surface. Start with a webcam or a supported phone setup. Choose the installer for your computer; no special dice or RollSight hardware purchase is required.

Use the free app with your own dice and connect your table. No card required.

[Download RollSight](https://www.rollsight.com/download) · [Compare game integrations](https://www.rollsight.com/integrations)

## Quick Start

1. Have the room owner add and enable RollSight using the extension manifest below.
2. Get your code from the room’s RollSight Connection tab. Enter it under Connect dice → Owlbear Rodeo in the desktop app.
3. In the room’s RollSight panel, choose your dice and click Request dice.
4. Roll, check the faces in RollSight and send. See the total in Owlbear and replays in Results.

https://www.rollsight.com/owlbear/manifest.json

[Setup guide](https://www.rollsight.com/guides/owlbear-rodeo)

Public rolls only. Forge applies damage or healing through its own controls. No universal sheet or automatic resource support. Corrections do not repeat game effects. Replay and RollStats require eligible plans and upload or sync.
